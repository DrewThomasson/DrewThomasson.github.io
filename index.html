Inside the E2A ecosystem

From five seconds of voice
to a deployable model.

I built a synthetic-data-assisted training pipeline around the Universal TTS Finetuner: clone a voice from a few seconds of reference audio, generate hours of clean teacher data with XTTS, then train a compact Piper model that runs faster and on far less hardware.
